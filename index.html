Features

Everything you need to prep a shoot

Built by a director, for directors. No subscription, no cloud, no fluff.

Screenplay import

Drop in your PDF and Director's Prep automatically parses scenes, characters, and dialogue — ready to block immediately.

Floorplan blocking

Place actors, cameras, props, and furniture on a top-down floorplan. Drag and rotate everything into position.

Camera timeline

Plan your edit before you shoot. Drop camera cuts onto the timeline to visualise which camera is live at every moment.

Keyframe animation

Set keyframes to show actor and camera movement through a scene. See exactly where everyone needs to be at every beat.

Export storyboard

Print or export your floorplan and storyboard frames as a PDF to share with your crew before the shoot.

Works offline

No internet required, no account, no subscription. Your prep files stay on your machine in a simple open format.

How it works

From script to shoot-ready in minutes

A simple workflow that fits how directors actually prep.

1

Import your screenplay

Drag your PDF onto the window. Director's Prep parses every scene and character automatically.

2

Block each scene

Place actors and cameras on the floorplan. Set keyframes to show movement through the scene.

3

Plan your edit

Use the camera timeline to drop in cuts. See your edit plan before you've shot a single frame.

4

Walk onto set

Export your floorplans and share with your AD and crew. Everyone knows the plan.
